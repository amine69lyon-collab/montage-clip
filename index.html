Montage Clip XelaisTV

A personal desktop tool used by the content creator XelaisTV (twitch.tv/xelaistv).

It turns the creator's own Twitch clips into short vertical videos and, after the creator has reviewed them, publishes them to the creator's own TikTok, Instagram and YouTube accounts. It is not offered to the public: its only user is its operator.
